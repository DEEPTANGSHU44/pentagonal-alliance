MEMBERSHIP OF THE PENTAGONAL ALLIANCE

Membership is an exceptional privilege, not an ordinary entitlement.

The Alliance has no obligation to expand its membership and may remain composed solely of its five founding members.

General Membership Standard

Admission to the Alliance shall be considered only where the candidate demonstrates exceptional merit, integrity, reliability, compatibility and the ability to contribute meaningfully to the Alliance.

Failure at any stage shall terminate the application process.

No automatic appeal shall arise from rejection.

Stage I — Nomination

A candidate shall not nominate themselves for membership.

A nomination may be submitted only by an existing founding member.

However, a nomination shall become formally eligible for consideration only when at least four of the five founding members agree to place the candidate before the Nomination Stage.

Nomination shall not constitute membership or create any membership rights.

Nomination Criteria

A candidate should satisfy the following standards:

  1. Integrity Threshold — Demonstrated honesty and trustworthy conduct.
  2. Alliance Compatibility Test — Ability to cooperate respectfully with all five founding members.
  3. Multi-Domain Merit Criterion — Demonstrated merit in at least three distinct fields.
  4. Contribution Record Requirement — Evidence of meaningful contribution to shared activities or projects.
  5. Reliability Standard — Consistent responsibility and dependability.
  6. Independent Merit Requirement — The candidate's achievements must reflect genuine individual merit.
  7. Charter Compatibility Requirement — Conduct and values must be consistent with the principles of the Alliance.
  8. Conflict-Management Criterion — Ability to handle disagreements constructively.
  9. Long-Term Contribution Test — Demonstrated potential to contribute beyond a single event or achievement.
  10. Exceptional Merit Threshold — The overall case must be sufficiently strong to justify consideration for membership.

Stage II — Scrutiny

Candidates who successfully pass the Nomination Stage may proceed to formal scrutiny.

Scrutiny shall constitute a substantially more rigorous examination of the candidate's character, conduct, contributions, compatibility and long-term value to the Alliance.

Where appropriate, the Council may observe the candidate's conduct over a period of time before reaching its conclusion.

Scrutiny Criteria

The following twelve examinations shall form the principal Scrutiny Standard:

  1. Five-Member Compatibility Examination — Assessment of the candidate's ability to work constructively with every founding member.
  2. Consistency Verification Test — Examination of whether the candidate's conduct consistently reflects the qualities presented during nomination.
  3. Integrity Verification — Detailed consideration of honesty, responsibility and ethical conduct.
  4. Contribution Sustainability Test — Assessment of whether the candidate can make meaningful contributions over time.
  5. Pressure & Adversity Assessment — Evaluation of conduct when facing disagreement, pressure, setbacks or difficult circumstances.
  6. Collective Responsibility Test — Assessment of whether the candidate can place collective objectives above unnecessary personal advantage.
  7. Intellectual & Creative Contribution Assessment — Examination of the candidate's ability to bring useful ideas, knowledge, creativity or expertise.
  8. Diplomatic Conduct Assessment — Evaluation of respect, communication and constructive behaviour toward others.
  9. Confidentiality & Trust Test — Assessment of whether the candidate can be trusted with appropriate private Alliance matters.
  10. Leadership Without Dominance Test — Assessment of whether the candidate can lead, contribute and take responsibility without attempting to dominate others.
  11. Adaptability Assessment — Evaluation of the candidate's ability to adapt to different activities, responsibilities and circumstances.
  12. Long-Term Value Assessment — Final consideration of whether the candidate would strengthen the Alliance over the long term.

Unanimity of Confidence Standard

Completion of the Scrutiny Stage shall require the independent confidence of every founding member.

Each of the five founding members must be satisfied that the candidate has demonstrated the qualities required for progression to the final stage.

If even one founding member cannot reasonably support the candidate's progression, the Scrutiny Stage shall be considered unsuccessful.

This standard exists because membership affects the entire Alliance and not merely an individual member.

Stage III — Final Admission

A candidate who successfully completes Scrutiny may be placed before the Founding Council for a final admission vote.

Each of the five founding members shall possess one vote.

Admission shall require:

5 / 5 — UNANIMOUS APPROVAL

Any negative vote, abstention or failure to participate shall prevent admission.

No Automatic Admission

No candidate shall acquire membership automatically through nomination, successful scrutiny, friendship, talent, participation, victories or popularity.

Membership shall exist only following the formal unanimous decision of the Founding Council.

The Membership Principle

Membership is not something a candidate is entitled to receive.

It is something the Alliance must unanimously conclude that the candidate deserves.

The Alliance must be stronger with the candidate than without them.

Formal Recognition of Membership

Following unanimous admission, the new member may be formally recognized by the Founding Council and shall affirm their commitment to the principles of the Pentagonal Alliance.

Membership shall thereafter be subject to the Charter and all applicable Alliance rules.
