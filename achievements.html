ACHIEVEMENTS & HISTORY

This page records the major achievements, victories, milestones and historical moments of the Pentagonal Alliance.

Every achievement is recorded not merely as a result, but as part of the institutional history of the Alliance.

Official Record of Achievements

The Founding Council shall maintain a continuing record of important achievements of the Pentagonal Alliance.

Each significant entry may include its date, field, participants, result and historical significance.

🏆 FIRST RECORDED ACHIEVEMENT

FOOTBALL VICTORY — 4–1

The Pentagonal Alliance recorded its first official achievement with a football victory by a score of 4–1.

The victory established the first competitive milestone in the history of the Alliance.

FIRST VICTORY — FIRST MILESTONE — ONE ALLIANCE

Historical Principle

The Alliance shall remember both victories and lessons. Achievement is valued not only for winning, but also for the effort, cooperation, discipline and learning behind it.

Every achievement belongs to the history of the Alliance and should be recorded with accuracy and fairness.

The Historical Record

The record will grow as the Pentagonal Alliance undertakes new competitions, projects and intellectual initiatives.

Future achievements may be added by decision of the Founding Council and preserved as part of the Alliance's official history.
