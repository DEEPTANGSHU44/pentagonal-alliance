Founding Declaration

The Pentagonal Alliance is established as a voluntary association of five equal founding members, united by friendship, cooperation and a shared pursuit of excellence.

The Alliance shall seek excellence not only in competition, but also in academics, science, creativity, oratory, culture and strategic thinking.

Its strength shall be measured not merely by its victories, but by the cooperation, trust and achievements of its members.
