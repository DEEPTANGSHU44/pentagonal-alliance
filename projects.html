JOINT PROJECTS & INITIATIVES

The Pentagonal Alliance encourages its members to work together on projects that develop knowledge, creativity, teamwork and practical ability.

Projects may involve one or more fields and may be temporary or long-term in nature.

Project Principles

Every official Alliance project should be consistent with the principles of the Charter.

  1. Clear purpose
  2. Defined participants
  3. Shared responsibilities
  4. Reasonable planning
  5. Cooperation and communication
  6. Recognition of contributors
  7. A clear outcome or objective

Official Project Proposal

A proposed official project should normally identify:

  1. The name of the project
  2. The purpose of the project
  3. The participating members
  4. The responsibilities of each participant
  5. The required resources
  6. The expected timetable
  7. The intended outcome

Recognition of Contribution

Contributions to Alliance projects should be recognized fairly. Research, planning, organization, creativity, leadership, strategy and encouragement are all valuable forms of contribution.

No single type of contribution shall automatically be considered more important than another.

Future Initiatives

The Projects Division is intended to grow with the Alliance. New initiatives may be proposed whenever members identify an opportunity for meaningful cooperation.
