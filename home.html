ABOUT THE ALLIANCE

The Pentagonal Alliance was established by five founding members who cooperate across multiple fields of activity.

The Alliance encourages sports, academics, science and technology, oratory and debate, arts and culture, strategy and games, and other constructive forms of collaboration.

Spain, Italy, the United Kingdom, Deutschland and France serve as symbolic identities within the Alliance. These identities do not constitute political allegiance to governments or political parties.

The Alliance is governed by its Charter and its valid amendments.
