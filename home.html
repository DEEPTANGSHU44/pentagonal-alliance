WELCOME

The Pentagonal Alliance is a five-member association founded upon friendship, cooperation, competition, intellectual curiosity and shared ambition. Its five founding members represent five symbolic national identities, forming the pentagonal foundation of the Alliance.

THE FOUNDING COUNCIL

The five founding members form the historical foundation of the Pentagonal Alliance. The present executive structure is headed by two Presidents, with a Deputy President assisting in administration and coordination.

EXECUTIVE AUTHORITY

The Pentagonal Alliance is headed by two Presidents: Deeptangshu Goldar and Anik Jana.

The Presidents constitute the highest executive authority of the Alliance.

Routine executive matters may be decided by either President individually.

Major decisions affecting the fundamental structure, membership, identity, Charter or long-term direction of the Alliance require the agreement of both Presidents.

Avirup Halder serves as Deputy President and assists the Presidents in administration and coordination.

ABOUT THE ALLIANCE

The Alliance brings together cooperation across sports, academics, science and technology, oratory and debate, arts and culture, strategy and other constructive fields. Its national identities are symbolic and represent the character and identity of its members. They do not constitute political allegiance to any government or political party.
