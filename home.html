FOUNDING DECLARATION

The Pentagonal Alliance is founded upon unity, mutual respect, excellence, fair play, intellectual curiosity, loyalty and responsibility.

Its five founding members stand as equals within the Founding Council and cooperate across sports, academics, science, technology, oratory, culture and strategy.

ABOUT THE PENTAGONAL ALLIANCE

The Pentagonal Alliance is a five-member association founded upon friendship, cooperation, competition and shared ambition.

Its five founding members adopt five symbolic national identities — Spain, Italy, the United Kingdom, Deutschland and France — forming a pentagonal structure of unity.

The national identities of the Alliance are symbolic and represent the character and identity of its members. They do not constitute political allegiance to any government or political party.

The Alliance operates through its Founding Council, in which all five founders possess equal standing and one equal vote.

Its activities extend across sports, academics, science and technology, oratory and debate, arts and culture, strategy and other areas of constructive cooperation.
