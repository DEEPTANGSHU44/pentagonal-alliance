THE FOUNDING COUNCIL

The Founding Council is the supreme deliberative body of the Pentagonal Alliance. It consists of the five original founders, each possessing equal standing and one equal vote.

The Council represents the principle: Five Members — One Council — Equal Voice.

Principle of Equal Voice

No founder possesses permanent superior authority over the others. Each member of the Founding Council has one vote.

The Alliance is founded upon equality, cooperation and mutual respect rather than hierarchy.

FIVE MEMBERS — ONE COUNCIL — EQUAL VOICE

Responsibilities of the Council

  1. Protect the principles of the Pentagonal Alliance.
  2. Make important decisions concerning the Alliance.
  3. Approve major projects and initiatives.
  4. Resolve internal disputes through reasoned dialogue.
  5. Consider applications for future membership.
  6. Maintain the Charter and approve amendments.
  7. Record important achievements and milestones.
  8. Plan the future development of the Alliance.

The Council's Authority

The authority of the Founding Council exists to serve the Alliance, not to establish personal dominance.

Decisions shall be made according to the procedures established by the Charter, with consensus preferred wherever possible.

Every founder retains the right to express an independent opinion, raise objections, propose initiatives and participate equally in Council deliberations.

The Fivefold Foundation

The five founders form the historical foundation of the Pentagonal Alliance.

Their different identities represent five distinct national symbols united within a single institutional framework.

The strength of the Alliance does not come from one individual. It comes from the cooperation of all five.
