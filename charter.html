CHARTER OF THE PENTAGONAL ALLIANCE

The foundational document governing the principles, institutions and cooperation of the Pentagonal Alliance.

Article I — Name

The organization shall be known as the Pentagonal Alliance.

The five founding members constitute the Founding Council of the Alliance.

Article II — Principles

The Alliance shall be founded upon the principles of:

  • Unity
  • Mutual Respect
  • Excellence
  • Fair Play
  • Intellectual Curiosity
  • Loyalty
  • Responsibility

Article III — Objectives

The Alliance shall seek to promote:

  • Excellence in sport and competition
  • Academic and intellectual development
  • Creative and cultural collaboration
  • Scientific and technological exploration
  • Friendship and cooperation among its members

Article IV — Founding Council

The Founding Council shall consist of the five founding members of the Pentagonal Alliance.

All founding members shall possess equal standing within the Council.

No permanent superior authority shall exist among the founding members.

Five Members — One Council — Equal Voice.

Article V — Decision-Making

The Alliance shall seek consensus wherever reasonably possible.

Where consensus cannot be reached, decisions may be determined by vote.

Each founding member shall possess one vote.

Ordinary decisions shall require a simple majority, while decisions concerning fundamental identity or the Charter shall require broader agreement as determined by the Council.

Article VI — Dispute Resolution

Disagreements shall be addressed through private discussion, reasoned dialogue and mutual respect.

Insults, threats, public humiliation and deliberate exclusion shall not constitute acceptable methods of resolving disputes.

Resolve the disagreement, preserve the friendship.

Article VII — Areas of Cooperation

The Alliance may cooperate in the following fields:

  • Sports
  • Cricket
  • Academics
  • Science & Technology
  • Oratory & Debate
  • Arts & Culture
  • Strategy & Games

Article VIII — Competition

Competition within the Alliance shall be conducted according to the principle of fair play.

Competition shall serve as a means of improvement rather than division.

Victories shall be celebrated without degrading opponents, and defeats shall be accepted without abandoning the principles of the Alliance.

Article IX — Identity

The national identities represented by the five founding members are symbolic representations of friendship, culture and identity.

They shall not constitute political allegiance to any government or political party.

Article X — Official Motto

FIVE NATIONS, ONE ALLIANCE.

Article XI — Amendments

This Charter may be amended by the Founding Council.

Any amendment shall preserve the fundamental principles of Unity, Respect, Fairness, Excellence and Cooperation.

Article XII — Founding Declaration

The Pentagonal Alliance is hereby formally recognized as an association of its five founding members.

Its first recorded achievement was a football victory by a score of 4–1.

The Alliance shall not be defined by a single victory, but by the continuing cooperation, friendship and achievements of its members.

Article XIII — Presidency of the Council

A Chairperson may be designated by the Founding Council for a specified period.

The Chairperson shall coordinate meetings, maintain order, ensure reasonable speaking opportunities and assist in implementing Council decisions.

The Chairperson shall possess no superior authority over the other founding members.

Article XIV — Secretariat

The Secretariat shall maintain the official records of the Alliance.

Such records may include the Charter, amendments, meeting minutes, decisions, competitions, projects and historical achievements.

Article XV — Official Meetings

Official meetings of the Alliance shall be known as Alliance Summits.

A Summit may be called to address important decisions, projects, disputes, new areas of cooperation or matters requested by a member.

Informal conversations shall not automatically constitute official Council decisions.

Article XVI — Agenda of the Summit

An Alliance Summit may include:

  1. Opening of the Summit
  2. Review of previous decisions
  3. Current affairs of the Alliance
  4. Presentation of proposals
  5. Discussion
  6. Decisions and voting
  7. Future plans
  8. Closing of the Summit

Article XVII — Joint Projects

The Alliance may undertake any project consistent with this Charter.

A project proposal should identify its purpose, participants, responsibilities, required resources, timetable and intended outcome.

Members who contribute substantially to a project shall receive appropriate recognition.

Article XVIII — Division of Responsibilities

Responsibilities may be assigned temporarily according to interest, ability and availability.

Responsibilities may include research, presentation, design, documentation, strategy and organization.

No permanent hierarchy shall arise solely from the distribution of responsibilities.

Article XIX — Principle of Contribution

Contributions to the Alliance may take many forms, including research, planning, organization, creativity, leadership and encouragement.

Both effort and achievement shall be valued.

Article XX — Information and Confidentiality

Members shall exercise reasonable discretion regarding private Alliance discussions.

Information specifically designated as confidential shall not be disclosed without the agreement of the relevant members.

Confidentiality shall never be used to conceal cheating, wrongdoing or conduct inconsistent with the Charter.

Article XXI — External Relations

The Alliance may cooperate with other groups, organizations or individuals.

Such cooperation shall not automatically constitute membership in the Pentagonal Alliance.

Temporary partnerships may be established for particular activities or projects.

Article XXII — Observer Status

A non-member may be invited to participate in a particular Summit or Alliance activity as an Observer.

Observers shall possess no voting rights unless such rights are separately granted by the Council.

Observer status shall not constitute membership.

Article XXIII — Membership

The five founding members constitute the original historical foundation of the Pentagonal Alliance.

The admission of any future member shall require a formal decision of the Founding Council.

Any future membership shall be subject to the identity, principles and requirements established by this Charter.

Article XXIV — Conduct

Every member shall uphold the dignity and principles of the Alliance.

Deliberate dishonesty, personal attacks, humiliation, misrepresentation and unnecessary hostility shall not be consistent with Alliance conduct.

Article XXV — Record of Achievements

The Alliance shall maintain an official Record of Achievements.

Each record may include the date, field, participants, result and significance of the achievement.

The first recorded achievement is the Football Victory by 4–1.

Article XXVI — Annual Review

The Alliance may conduct an annual review of its achievements, projects, difficulties, cooperation and future objectives.

The review may also consider amendments and improvements to the functioning of the Alliance.

Article XXVII — Symbols

The Alliance may establish official symbols including a flag, coat of arms or emblem, seal, ceremonial music, colours and insignia.

Official symbols shall require approval by the Founding Council.

Article XXVIII — Official Declarations

The Alliance may issue official declarations concerning its founding, victories, projects, agreements and significant milestones.

Article XXIX — Emergency Council

Where an urgent matter requires immediate attention, the Founding Council may convene an Emergency Council.

An Emergency Council shall address only the urgent matter for which it was convened.

Ordinary Alliance procedures shall resume once the emergency matter has been addressed.

Article XXX — Continuity

The Alliance shall not be considered dissolved merely because a particular project, competition or event has concluded.

Its continuation shall depend upon the recognition and cooperation of its members.

This Charter shall remain the foundational document of the Alliance unless formally amended.

Article XXXI — Final Principle

The Pentagonal Alliance exists because five individuals voluntarily choose to cooperate as equals.

Its strength shall be measured not merely by its size, but by the cooperation, friendship, achievements and principles of its members.

Five Members — One Council — Equal Voice.
